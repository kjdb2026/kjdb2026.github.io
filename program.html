December 11–13, 2026. A welcome reception, two keynotes, three research sessions, and a panel discussion.

Program overview

Workshop program

Preliminary schedule

The workshop runs December 11–13, 2026. The main program is scheduled for December 12–13.

Session chairs, the moderator, and one panelist are to be announced. Times and details are subject to change.

All times are in Korea Standard Time (KST, UTC+9), the same as Japan Standard Time (JST).

For program enquiries, contact the Program Co-Chairs.
