About

Korea and Japan's annual workshop for database research.

The Korea-Japan (Japan-Korea) Database Workshop is an annual joint workshop with scholars in Korea, Japan, and neighboring research communities for sharing the latest activities in database and database-related technologies.

KJDB 2026 will continue the workshop tradition of interactive discussion, early research exchange, and collaboration across data management, AI, information retrieval, data mining, and emerging database systems.
