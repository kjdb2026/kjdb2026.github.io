Travel

Getting to the venue

Incheon Airport (ICN)

Airport limousine bus 6005

Take bus 6005 from Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 and get off at Fraser Place Central. The operator also lists a Fraser Place Namdaemun stop, so check the hotel name before getting off.

The published adult fare is KRW 17,000. Check your terminal's boarding location and the current timetable before travelling; journey times depend on traffic.

6005 route and timetable Airport boarding locations

Gimpo Airport (GMP)

Subway Line 5 to Seodaemun

Take Line 5 from Gimpo Airport toward central Seoul and get off at Seodaemun Station. Leave through Exit 6 and follow the map to the hotel, approximately a seven-minute walk.

Gimpo Airport subway guide

Rail connections

Via Seoul Station

The AREX airport railway connects Incheon Airport with Seoul Station. From Seoul Station, take a taxi to the hotel, or take Subway Line 1 to City Hall and use Exit 10.

Seoul's airport transport guide

The final walk

Nearby subway exits

  • Seodaemun, Line 5: Exit 6, about seven minutes on foot.
  • City Hall, Lines 1 and 2: Exit 10, about seven minutes on foot.

These walking estimates are from the hotel. If you need a step-free route or airport pickup, contact the hotel before arrival.
