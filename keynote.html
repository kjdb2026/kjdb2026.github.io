About the speaker

Jae-Gil Lee is a Professor in the School of Computing at KAIST and leads its Data Mining Lab. His research covers data-centric AI, deep learning for big data, mobility and stream data mining, and distributed deep learning.

Before joining KAIST, he held research positions at IBM Almaden Research Center and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.
